01 / New / Current Project · Civil Engineering
In Development • 2026

FutureLand

I’m building an interactive development-impact visualizer around one question: what happens if we build here? FutureLand is designed to model proposed residential, commercial and mixed-use development and show how it could change water demand, roads, schools, infrastructure, housing, communities, wildlife and natural resources.

The goal is not to produce a single score. It is to make tradeoffs visible — helping people see where development may make sense, where infrastructure would need to grow, and where water, environmental or community constraints create real red flags.

Care Technology

Grandma.care ↗

Built + Entered 2026
2:20 Demo

A two-sided caregiving platform Nick built to make everyday independence simpler for an older adult while giving family members an easy way to help from a distance.

  • Simple senior-facing tablet with reminders, family notes and clear “I did it” / “I need help” responses.
  • Caregiver dashboard for scheduling, missed-reminder alerts and family communication.
  • Designed around accessibility, low-friction interaction and preserving independence.
  • Entered in the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.

The next evolution of Grandma.care — expanding the same idea beyond one grandmother to other adults who may benefit from a little day-to-day support without giving up independence.
